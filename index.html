GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

My professional journey has taken me from SaaS technical support toward software quality assurance and full-stack development. With a background in technical troubleshooting, specialized training in IT support and software testing, and ongoing studies in web development, I'm focused on building the technical knowledge and practical skills needed to contribute to creating reliable, user-focused software.

My Background

My journey into technology began in the fashion industry, where I gained experience working with 2D and 3D apparel design tools. This experience eventually led me to a SaaS company specializing in 3D apparel design solutions, where I transitioned into a Software Support Agent role.

Working directly with software users gave me practical experience in technical troubleshooting, investigating software issues, and understanding how product reliability affects the user experience. I also collaborated with development and product teams to investigate reported issues, identify potential defects, and support improvements to the software.

Expanding My Technical Knowledge

My experience in technical support sparked a deeper interest in understanding how software works, how technical issues are diagnosed, and how software quality can be improved. To build on that foundation, I pursued specialized training in IT support and software quality assurance.

I completed the Microsoft IT Support Specialist certification program through Coursera, developing knowledge of computer hardware and software, networking, cybersecurity, data backup, Microsoft 365, and technical diagnostics and troubleshooting.

I also completed the Testing Mobile/Web Applications course, where I developed knowledge and practical skills in software testing across web and mobile applications. My training covered test planning and execution, functional and regression testing, API and database validation, test automation with Selenium WebDriver and Cucumber, and defect management using Jira.

These learning experiences broadened my technical perspective and strengthened my understanding of IT support and software quality assurance, complementing my professional background in SaaS technical support.

Continuing Toward Full-Stack Development

I'm currently expanding my skills through full-stack web development training, learning to build applications across the front end, back end, and database layers. My studies include HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express.js, RESTful APIs, SQL, and PostgreSQL, along with version control, application deployment, and web security.

I'm particularly interested in understanding how different technologies and application components work together to deliver functional, reliable, and intuitive software. Through my training and personal projects, I'm continuing to develop practical programming and problem-solving skills.

As my technical knowledge continues to grow, I'm looking forward to opportunities where I can combine my SaaS support experience, software testing knowledge, and developing programming skills. My goal is to contribute to building dependable software while continuing to grow as a technology professional.
